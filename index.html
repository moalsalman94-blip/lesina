FAQ

Good to know

Which products are right for my hair?

The shampoo and conditioner are made for all hair types. The hair mask is designed for dry and damaged hair, and the hair oil adds nourishment and shine to any routine.

In what order should I use them?

Cleanse with the shampoo, follow with the conditioner, treat with the hair mask, then finish with a few drops of hair oil.

What sizes are the products?

Shampoo and conditioner come in 500 ml bottles. The hair mask and hair oil come in 250 ml.

How do I place an order?

Add products to your bag, fill in your delivery details, then send the order to us on WhatsApp with one tap. We'll contact you to confirm your address and delivery time.
